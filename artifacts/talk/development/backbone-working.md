## Some initial working definitions (proposed)

- **Flattening:** Producing smooth transitions between representations without encountering some of the constraints that shaped or could shape those transitions outside the representational space.

- **Cognitive sovereignty:** The ability to recognize, interrupt, and redirect representational transitions before thir consequences become embedded in subsequent reasoning.

- **Flattening as an unavoidable consequence of representation:** Flattening is an unavoidable condition of representation. GenAI can change its cumulative consequences and opportunities to mediate them.

## Five proposition for the Talk Backbone

1. **Representations are product of mediation**. Data and language reflect obwervations classifications, interpretations, and decisions; not unmediated access to reality. Flattening is a necessary consequence of selective representation, not inherently a defect.

2. **Friction is constitutive, not merely corrective**. Encounters with constrains, competing interpretations, and unexpected signals help determine which representations emerge. Their absence can change subsequent trajectories.

3. **GenAI changes the conditions of mediation**. It's differently constituted representational environment can alter what forms of friction participate in shaping subsequent representations. GenAI can accelerate cumulative flattening with hybrid workflows.

4. **Reasoning externalization creates opportunities, not guarantees**. When governed appropriately it can support uncertainty communication, cognitive sovereignty, and collective scrutiny. When poorly governed, it can produce documentation overload and additional dependencies. The externalized object emerges through human-AI mediation.

5. Data equity includes meaningful access to mediation. The question is not only who can access representations, but who has opportunities to understand, contest, and influence how they develop and are used.

## Message Box Draft for Talk Backbone

The message box is a science communication tools designed by COMPASS Science Communication (<https://www.compassscicomm.org/leadership-development/the-message-box/>) that allows for an structured and brief map of the presentation content. It's main components include the problem, so-what?, solution, and benefits.

The following message box describe the backbone of the talk, it's content will be refined to link it to the TAPs example.

### Problem

GenAI can accelerate the cumulative flattening of representations connecting data to actions, potentially reducing opportunities for meaningful human mediation of consequential omissions and transformations.

### So What?

An illusion of "fast", "smart", "logical", and "coherent" outputs obscuring consequential omissions and transformations that emerged through the hybrid process including human choices.

### Solution

Reasoning externalization supported by appropriate governance as one approach to documenting consequential transitions in human-AI reasoning, creating opportunities for productive friction, human scrutiny, and intervention before resulting interpretations inform actions.

### Benefits

Make the hybrid workflow more inspectable, contestable, and governable, preserving meaningful human agency over resulting actions.
