## Emerging narrative direction — October 21

Organizer feedback, as reported by the author, suggests foregrounding the questions, reasoning, and judgments behind technology decisions rather than implementation detail. A candidate WRIS case may show how a data-development question grew from schema and spatial structure toward what records and query results mean and which population is represented. This could make the stakes legible without requiring a database deep dive; the specific episode and its source evidence remain to be selected and checked.

The author is exploring Philosophy of Technology as a conceptual home for questions about how technology shapes knowledge, understanding, assumptions, and responsibility. The talk is not intended to be a philosophy lecture. Treat this as a provisional lens that may support the practical narrative, not as an adopted theory or central message.
