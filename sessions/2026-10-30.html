Representation, navigation, mediation and externalization

  • feeds: knowledge-base/01
  • status: open

The author clarified that navigation through representations means the processes applied to an initial set of representations as they evolve toward the most current one. It includes transformation and revision, rather than only movement among existing artifacts. The assistant had initially interpreted navigation too narrowly. Most current does not necessarily mean better or more accurate; development can branch, revisit earlier representations or leave alternatives unresolved.

The assistant introduced current understanding as the destination of this development. The author questioned that wording because it may not apply to GenAI, and emphasized that the full representational space of a GenAI mediator cannot be accessed or fully externalized. This is a conceptual position to refine: representational space, access and the scope of the limitation require clarification before technical assertion. No new empirical or architectural claim was verified here.

Concept Provisional distinction for review
Representation A selective configuration or account; the discussion includes representations that have not been externally expressed. Human internal representations and model representations should not be assumed equivalent.
Navigation through representations Processes through which an initial set is transformed, combined, questioned or revised into subsequent representations. Cognitive processes can describe the human contribution; extending that description to model operations remains unresolved.
Mediation How an encounter and its conditions shape the relationship with what is being addressed, including how representations develop and are used. The proposed relationship between navigation and mediation requires scholarly checking; navigation is not asserted to be Ihde’s terminology.
Externalization Making selected aspects of representations, transitions or their rationale externally available for examination. Whether this term covers both artifact production and preservation of developmental rationale remains open.

The accessible interaction record can preserve prompts, responses, revisions and selected rationale. It should not be treated as a complete account of human cognition or model operations. A generated response is externally available, but describing it as an externalization of model reasoning would imply a correspondence not established in this discussion. Likewise, a polished explanation can express current rationale without preserving the actual developmental sequence or alternatives.

Possible presentation implication, not adopted wording: examine how representations develop through mediated interaction while recognizing that the record offers partial access to what shaped their development. Documentation can be described as a selected record of that development. Questions include what becomes available for scrutiny, what remains inaccessible and who can assess consequential transitions. These distinctions are project discussion proposals, not definitions attributed to Ihde or verified claims about GenAI.
